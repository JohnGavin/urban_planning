Exam Structure

Part A: Subject Knowledge

Weight: 40%

OEREK 2030 + Studienplan content. Multiple choice questions testing factual knowledge of Austrian spatial planning.

Part B: Text Comprehension

Weight: 20%

Read German texts on spatial planning topics. Assess if statements are true, false, or not determinable from the text.

Part C: Cognitive Abilities

Weight: 40%

Analogies, number sequences, logic tasks, spatial reasoning, and matrix pattern recognition.

Good news: In 2024 and 2025, fewer people participated in the Reihungstest than there were places available (189/200 and 182/200). This means currently everyone who participates and does reasonably well gets a place. However, this could change in 2026 with the new earlier registration deadline.
